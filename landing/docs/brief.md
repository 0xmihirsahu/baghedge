# landing brief

the landing page for coldhedge. the product itself is in ../brief.md, read that first.

what. one marketing page, no app behind it yet. the call to action is the waitlist, there's nothing to log into.

who. hyperliquid curious holders who keep coins in a ledger, metamask or phantom, plus the near hackathon judges.

the one line. hedge the crypto in your own wallet.

must do. explain the product in two seconds, show the real product moment (draw a hedge on a live chart, see exposure change), and be honest that it's a hedge and not insurance.

data. live hyperliquid prices, candles and funding, pulled in the browser (the info api allows any origin). every number on the page is live, sourced, or labelled as a sample. no invented stats.

look. reference is mochatrade.com for the story shape (problems told with physical cards, then the product as real ui) and olivermrose's sites for motion. palette is cold, not mocha's orange. no glows, no gradients, real token logos, phosphor icons, capitalised ui labels, a real desktop layout.

assets. the product loop film is made in the video harness (/Users/viveksahu/Documents/videos/video-harness) and lands in `web/public/media/`.

out of scope. login, trading, real hedges, a blog, analytics.

runs on localhost:3300 because musdy holds 3100.
