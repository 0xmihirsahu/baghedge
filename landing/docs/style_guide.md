# style guide

reference is phantom.com (screens in refs/phantom/). we take its grammar, none of its content. our art is the halftone set in web/public/art/, our mascot is the red bag.

## colour

phantom runs on lavender, we run on the red from the art. page `#f6f3ee`, ink `#2a2321`, brand `#f0645a`, brand tint `#fde3df` for pills, deep section `#3b2321` (phantom's dark purple), hero card `#121212`. card fills rotate through brand red, `#1c1c1c`, cream `#ece8e1`, blush `#fbe3df` and butter `#f7efc9`. the page colour changes per section and the nav recolours with it.

## type

figtree, regular weight for headlines like phantom (400, -0.025em). hero headline 80px, section headlines 80px centred with the mascot sitting inside the sentence, card text 30px at 1.2 leading. pills and nav 15 to 16px.

## shape

rounded, the way phantom is. hero card radius 28 inset 48px from the page. cards 427 by 570, radius 24, 48px padding, text top left, one visual filling the bottom. pills fully round. a dark chip with an icon names each carousel, round arrow buttons sit on the right.

## motion

the hero's shapes drift slowly behind the type (a thick grey price line, a red disc, a cream oval). headlines and cards rise in once. carousels scroll horizontally with snap and arrow buttons. the nav recolours as sections pass under it. nothing bounces.

## art

halftone objects (white dots on dark and red cards, ink dots on light cards) stand for real parts of the product. stickers and ticket stubs are drawn in code with real labels ("Hedge below", "Stop above"). the phone screen shows the live SOL price from hyperliquid.
